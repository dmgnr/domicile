Electron apps hear the machine suspend, resume and shut down from logind, held off with delay locks as Electron takes them. `systemPreferences.getAccentColor` and `accent-color-changed` follow the settings portal's accent, and `net.isOnline` reads the links Chromium does. `app` says `browser-window-created`, `browser-window-focus`, `browser-window-blur`, `session-created`, `will-finish-launching` and `child-process-gone` for utility processes, and `webContents` says `ipc-message` and `ipc-message-sync`. A `net.request` to a `host` with a port reaches that port, and an aborted response says `aborted`.
