Right-click a video for "Picture in picture", and drag a picture-in-picture video's progress bar to move through it. Shells get the menu's `picture-in-picture` action with `canPictureInPicture` / `inPictureInPicture`, and `pictureInPicture.seek(id, seconds)`.
