The tray has a menu of every icon at its end. Pin or unpin any app or extension icon, switch extensions on and off, and change what each can do: its options, which websites it can read, private windows, local files and user scripts, or uninstall it. Settings your config sets show locked. Shells get the same through `domicile.extensions.installed`, `setEnabled`, `configure` and `uninstall`. `Menu` holds check, radio and submenu items, and `ListRow` takes `actions` beside a pressed row.
