Email links can be written from a Gmail alias. `gmailInbox` lists the account's verified "Send mail as" addresses, and writing from one saves the message as a draft from it, then opens that draft. This needs the `gmail.settings.basic` and `gmail.compose` scopes, so each Gmail account signs in again once.
