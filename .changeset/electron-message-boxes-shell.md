Manganese shows an Electron app's message box over the window that asked, with the app's buttons in its order. Enter presses the app's default button and Escape its cancel button, and the app's checkbox, such as "Don't ask again", is a switch. `@domicile-desktop/sdk/message-box` reads a window's `messageBox` for any shell.
