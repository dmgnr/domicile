Electron apps get `Menu` and `MenuItem`. `Menu.buildFromTemplate` builds menus with Electron's role labels and accelerators, role menus such as `editMenu`, checkboxes, radios, icons and submenus, and changes to a menu are heard by what draws it. `setApplicationMenu` and `getApplicationMenu` keep the app's menu; the menu bar and the tray that draw these come next.
