Manganese draws an Electron app's menu bar under its window's tab. The app's accelerators run their entries, Alt and a menu's letter open it, and Alt shows or hides a bar the app auto-hides. The SDK reads a window's menu with `@domicile-desktop/sdk/app-menu` (`appMenu`, `acceleratedBy`, `openedByMnemonic`), and the component library has a `MenuBar`.
